Frequently asked questions

How do I buy?

Add your items to the cart and tap Pay: checkout happens on Stripe's secure page with your card. When you finish, you'll receive a confirmation email with your order number.

How much is shipping?

It depends on your order total: under $200 → $12 · $200 to $999 → $25 · $1,000 or more → $39. Shipping is always insured and signature-required, and takes 2 to 5 business days.

Can I pick up in person?

Yes. At checkout choose 📍 Pickup — it's free — and we'll email you to arrange the day and time in Miami, Florida.

How do I track my order?

With the 📦 "Order status" button at the top of this page, using your order number and your email. When your order ships, the tracking link appears right there.

Are the items authentic?

Yes. Every piece is inspected and verified before it is listed, and sold exactly as shown in the photos. These are one-of-a-kind items: when they sell, they're gone from the store.

What if my order arrives damaged or different from the description?

Write to us with your order number as soon as you receive it and we'll resolve it with you directly. Keep the box and packaging until we close the case.

Do you ship outside the United States?

For now we only ship within the United States.

Can I leave a review of my purchase?

Of course! When your order is delivered, you'll get an email with the link to leave your review. You can also write it on the item's page, in the "Customer comments" section.
